# Naming and product map

Status: working names pending trademark clearance

## Canonical names

| Name | Role | Ownership target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NetCityOS | Enterprise platform and product family | ООО «НетСити»; exact legal name must be confirmed |
| KVP | Kernel Validation Protocol and its reference implementation | ООО «НетСити» as right holder/applicant, subject to chain of title |
| Architect OS | NetCityOS architecture-design and composition subsystem | ООО «НетСити» as right holder |
| NetCityOS Enterprise Workspace | Operator-facing interface and scenario environment | ООО «НетСити» as right holder |
| KVP Ready | Proposed adapter/provider compatibility mark | Trademark and certification-policy review required |

`KVP` is the canonical protocol abbreviation in this repository. Variants such
as `QVP`, `NAPCity OS`, or alternative spellings are not introduced into code or
applications until the founders approve an intentional rename and clearance
search. The exact Russian and English corporate name, INN/OGRN, address, and
signing authority remain placeholders in legal drafts.

## Product boundaries

- **NetCityOS** is the complete Enterprise system.
- **Enterprise Workspace** is the human/operator experience.
- **Architect OS** models, validates, versions, and deploys architectures.
- **Runtime & Tool Fabric** executes governed sessions, workflows, and tools.
- **Fleet Control Plane** manages local and cloud model capacity.
- **KVP** transports and reconciles protected control/evidence operations.
- **Adapters and connectors** translate KVP/platform semantics to concrete local
  engines and cloud APIs.

## Naming rules

Public claims always distinguish platform guarantees from provider guarantees.
The names `KVP`, `NetCityOS`, `Architect OS`, and `KVP Ready` require separate
availability and registrability searches before trademark applications. Domain,
package, repository, binary, and legal-deposit names must reference the same
canonical naming decision record.
